# Creative Profile

## Role
Route creative tasks to brand/product rules and only the workflows needed for execution.

## Product visual priorities
For the Polo Piquet, emphasize three-dimensional piquet texture. Side light is desirable. Protect black-product detail and control color temperature for Marrom Mogno.

## Content architecture
Combine:
`Brand Rules + Creative Principles + Content Workflow + Channel/Format Reference`.

Do not load every creative reference for every task.

## Creative intelligence
A validated creative may be analyzed for hook, structure, first seconds, framing, rhythm, duration, text, caption, CTA, positioning, offer, storytelling, product, person/influencer, movement, scene changes, music, sound design, editing, colors, typography, graphic elements, sales pattern, retention pattern, and recurrence among winners.

Classification ladder:
`OBSERVATION → HYPOTHESIS → RECURRING_PATTERN → VALIDATED_PATTERN → CREATIVE_RULE`.

A single occurrence must never become a brand rule.
