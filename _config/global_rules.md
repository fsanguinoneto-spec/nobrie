# Global Rules

1. Do not invent Nobrie facts.
2. Prefer canonical sources and preserve provenance.
3. Load minimum sufficient context for each task.
4. Keep current state separate from history.
5. Record meaningful decisions explicitly.
6. Isolate projects by default.
7. Do not promote temporary outputs to durable knowledge automatically.
8. After meaningful work, assess whether the workspace requires synchronization.
9. Preserve superseded decisions and historical records rather than silently deleting them.
10. Keep documents semantically dense and operationally useful.
