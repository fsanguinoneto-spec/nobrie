# Creative Analysis Workflow

## Purpose
Extract reusable patterns from creatives already classified as valid.

## Process
1. Confirm the creative is valid/eligible for analysis.
2. Inspect hook, structure, first seconds, framing, rhythm, duration, text, caption, CTA, positioning, offer, storytelling, product, person/influencer, movement, scene changes, music, sound design, editing, colors, typography, graphic elements, sales pattern, and retention pattern.
3. Classify findings as `OBSERVATION`, `HYPOTHESIS`, `RECURRING_PATTERN`, `VALIDATED_PATTERN`, or `CREATIVE_RULE`.
4. Require recurrence/evidence before promoting an observation into a rule.
5. Store durable learnings in the creative-intelligence knowledge area when they are sufficiently validated.

## Principle
Validated creatives are evidence. They do not automatically become brand rules.
