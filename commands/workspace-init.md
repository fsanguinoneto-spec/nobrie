# /workspace-init

Initialize or validate the Nobrie workspace operating system.

1. Verify `AGENTS.md`, `CONTEXT.md`, `WORKSPACE_STATE.md`, and `CHANGELOG.md`.
2. Verify global configuration under `_config/`.
3. Verify reusable procedures and templates.
4. Confirm project isolation and canonical-source rules.
5. Confirm no Nobrie-specific facts have been invented during bootstrap.
6. Record the initial architecture change in the changelog.

Infrastructure comes before company-specific onboarding.
